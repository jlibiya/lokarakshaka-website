Our Story

Lokarakshaka Hospital was founded with a single, clear purpose: to bring world-class medical care closer to the communities that need it most. What began as a modest clinic has grown into a full-service multi-speciality hospital, serving over one lakh patients across 25 years.


Our name — Lokarakshaka, meaning 'Protector of the People' — reflects the promise we made at the very beginning. Every decision we take is guided by that promise.
